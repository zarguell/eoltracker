Hardware lifecycle catalog

Hardware end-of-sale and end-of-support dates

{{ plural(hardware_count, 'hardware record') }} across {{ plural(hardware_vendors|length, 'vendor') }}. Two record kinds are published together and deliberately kept apart: lifecycle rows, each one a published row of a vendor notice table carrying the end-of-sale and end-of-support dates its source states, and catalog records, each one exact model string the vendor's own product catalogue lists, which carry a listing state rather than a support claim. The catalog is normalized from {{ hardware_source_name }}, which aggregates public vendor announcements, and from Opengear's end-of-life product list and {{ catalog_source_name }}; no date here is estimated.

Status

For eosl.date records, status comes from the row the model is published under. For Opengear lifecycle rows it is computed from the published support deadline against the import date, since that source states no row status. Status unknown is deliberate and neutral: a record with no deadline and no matching notice is shown as unknown rather than as supported, because a catalogue listing and a revision-only notice both say nothing about support. None of these is a vendor contract: individual exceptions may still apply.

Source and attribution

{{ hardware_source_attribution }} eosl.date states no license for that data, so it is attributed here and on every record page rather than relicensed. Opengear lifecycle dates are read from Opengear's end-of-life product list and its catalogue models from {{ catalog_source_site }}, and both are credited to Opengear; attribution is not a licence for either source, and no source materials are relicensed here. Scope
One record per published source row: a model or model group from eosl.date, an Opengear product group whose part numbers are grouped rather than expanded into one record each, or one exact Opengear catalogue model string. Record counts are not device counts.
