How each milestone is derived for hardware

General availability

First availability, when the source publishes a release or launch date. Opengear does not publish GA in its lifecycle tables.

End of sale

Last day sold: eosl.date's end-of-life-date column or Opengear's End of Sale / Old Part Sales End.

End of security support

No separate security-support deadline is normalized from these sources. Opengear contract exceptions remain in raw policy notes.

End of support

Published support end: eosl.date's EOSL/LDOS or Opengear's End of Support / Old Part Support Ends. Individual contract exceptions may apply.

Unknown date

Palo Alto Networks hardware publishes no value for that milestone. A missing date is an absent announcement, not a claim that support ends or continues.
