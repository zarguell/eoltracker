End of sale

End of security support

No separate security-support deadline is normalized from these sources. Opengear contract exceptions remain in raw policy notes.

End of support

Unknown date

NETGEAR publishes no value for that milestone. A missing date is an absent announcement, not a claim that support ends or continues.
