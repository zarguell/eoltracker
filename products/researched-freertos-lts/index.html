How to read these dates

The milestone meanings below are the catalog's shared vocabulary. The endoflife.date label rules do not apply to this record: stated dates come from the quoted vendor notices, while any derived date carries its own explicit vendor rule, base, source, and calculation.

General availability

First public release. Taken from the upstream releaseDate.

End of sale

Last day new licenses or units are sold. Only mapped from an upstream field whose label names an end of sale.

End of security support

Last day security fixes are published. Mapped from eolFrom or eoesFrom only when the label names security support.

End of life

End of all support. A published extended-support date wins; otherwise eolFrom when its label describes a full support end.

Blank date

The quoted notice publishes no value for that milestone. A missing date is an absent announcement, not a claim that support ends or continues.

Month precision

A date shown as a month (YYYY-MM) is the width the source published, not a truncated day. This catalog never pads a month with an invented day, so such a date is not syndicated to the day-precision feeds and is excluded from the OpenEoX export; both exclusions are published with their reasons.
